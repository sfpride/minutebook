# Membership Meetings

Minutes from annual and special meetings of the SF Pride membership.

Per the [Bylaws](https://sfpride.github.io/bylaws/1.2-Bylaws.html), the annual membership meeting is held each year for:
- Election of Directors
- Presentation of annual reports
- Other membership business
