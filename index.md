# SF Pride Minutebook

The official public record of San Francisco Pride's board and committee meetings.

---

## About This Archive

This digital minutebook provides transparent access to the governance records of the San Francisco Lesbian, Gay, Bisexual, Transgender Pride Parade and Celebration Committee (SF Pride). 

Every entry is:
- ✅ **Verified** — GPG-signed commits ensure authenticity
- 📜 **Immutable** — Git history provides a tamper-evident record
- 🔍 **Searchable** — Full-text search across all documents
