## New Business

### Document Review Process
- President Pham addressed access and viewing of documents, specifically regarding the Budget & Finance Committee and eliminating barriers to access. Emphasized the goal of transparency, noting that the process followed for the recent budget aligned with established process and policy ("process vs. preference"), while remaining careful regarding how sensitive topics and materials are shared.
- Treasurer Louie shared that remote options have been expanded to assist with access barriers, and extended an open invitation to join the Budget & Finance Committee (meeting regularly on the second Tuesday of odd-numbered months).
- Director Smith noted that an annual timeline is being created to outline month-to-month expectations (currently in process).

### Board Retreat
