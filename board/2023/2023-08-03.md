## New Business

### Board Meeting Protocol
- President Pham expressed finding joy in the monthly cadence.
- The board discussed protocol regarding procedure when there is no old or new business on the agenda.
- **Proposal:** That if there is no new/old business, the board may excuse themselves and the President will facilitate.
- **Consensus:** Reached on Board Meeting Protocol.

### Virtual AGM

- **Consensus:** Reached to approve a Virtual AGM.

### Ad Hoc IT Committee
- **Consensus:** Reached regarding the Ad Hoc IT Committee.

### Ad Hoc Equity Committee (Exploratory Committee)
*Focus: Emergence / Dynamic / A queerer lens and liberation*

**Discussion:**
- **The Personal:** Reflections on working in previous coalitions, but not at the board level.
- **As a Board:** Discussion on honoring labor (optional stipend/honorarium) and examining precedent at other organizations (e.g., Leather & LGBTQ Cultural District and others).
- Acknowledged that as an organization SF Pride is currently cash poor.
- Setting group work timeline for 90 days.
- Question raised regarding how to collaborate with Policy and Procedures (P&P) to shift policy to support findings.
- President Pham expressed support and remarked on the Policy and Procedures article.
- Director J. Smith remarked on the privilege inherent in free labor and volunteering.
- Director Wysinger raised how board compensation might reflect back to the community.
- Marsha Levine shared information regarding the 49% rule.
- Director Perez discussed dreaming and building, public perception of this initiative, suggested the naming "Board Compensation", and expressed curiosity regarding how this might shift board makeup.
- Executive Director Ford expressed concern regarding optics regarding funding from the City.
- Discussed clarifying the committee name while moving forward with its purpose.
- **Action:** Tabled.
