## New Business

### Election of Board Treasurer
The office of Treasurer was previously held by Suzanne Ford, who vacated the seat to serve as Interim Executive Director.

President Wysinger opened the floor for nominations:
- President Carolyn Wysinger nominated Director Janelle Luster; seconded by Director Spring Collins.
- Director Janelle Luster nominated Director Spring Collins; seconded by Director Di'ara Reid.

Board members cast votes anonymously.

**Result:** Director Janelle Luster was elected as Treasurer.

### Parade and Exhibitor Registration
Interim Executive Director Suzanne Ford presented an update on registration for the Parade and Exhibitors, opening this week. 
- New this year is an ask to add $1,000 per sponsorship level, which will be allocated to non-profit organizations.
- Board members are requested to send suggestions for organizations to support.
