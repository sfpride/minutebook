## Executive Director's Report

Executive Director Lopez provided a verbal overview of his written report included in the meeting packet:

- **RJB (Rachel Jacob Barnett)** shared an update on impending event registrations and sponsorships, expressing excitement for June 2022 and emphasizing efforts to sustain the organization year-round through relationships with individual donors.
- Due in part to rising COVID cases, Executive Director Lopez noted that opening sales now for an event 5 months away did not seem like the right move.
- Referencing the cash flow forecast in the packet, he noted that the organization may soon need to utilize its reserve account, line of credit, or another revenue source. February and March will be challenging due to expenses exceeding anticipated income.
- Projections remain conservative; if sales take off once registration opens, the situation could improve. Drawdowns are anticipated to be under $200k, with potential repayment beginning by May or June from parade and exhibitor revenues.
- Expanded on the meeting held with Treasurer Ford and a city supervisor, sharing that SF Pride would be interested in additional support from the City, possibly on an annual basis.
- RFPs have been issued for production, the community space survey has been distributed, and staff are preparing plans for the year. A full budget will be presented for review soon.

---

## Director Discussion

Director Smith thanked Executive Director Lopez and shared concerns:
1. Since the city and state are not shutting down, the moral compass leans on SF Pride to make the right health decisions for more challenged communities.
2. Avoiding the perception that Black and Brown women's stages and venues will take a hit due to financial constraints.
3. Exploring how to engage and leverage relationships with the City through Carolyn Wysinger, Lisa Williams, or George Smith.
4. Requesting monthly sponsorship reports showing monthly goals compared to actual incoming revenue.
5. Exploring opportunities to leverage demographic targeting through community stages and venues to benefit the stages, sponsors, the City, and SF Pride.

Executive Director Lopez responded:
- The community deserves a celebration, and the organization should be in decent shape in 5 months.
- Confident and excited about the community spaces program, noting there will be 10 opportunities for stages (matching 2019 levels).
- The criteria and process center Black and Brown women, trans people, gender non-conforming people, and two-spirited individuals: *"It may look different than before, and that isn't necessarily bad, it is evidence of us growing and changing and evolving in the situation we've been given."*

President Wysinger noted that SF Pride has improved over the last two years in sharing its sponsor story while keeping community safety at the forefront.

Director Smith noted his questions were prompted by another organization he is involved with weighing the importance of maintaining queer spaces amidst vaccinations, boosters, and pandemic concerns.
