## Executive Director's Report

Executive Director Lopez provided a verbal overview of his written report:

- **RJB** shared excitement for June 2022 and focus on sustaining the organization year-round through relationships with individual donors
- Given rising COVID cases, opening sales 5 months before the event didn't seem right
- Referencing the cash flow forecast, soon the organization may need to use its reserve account or line of credit
- February and March will be challenging with more expenses than anticipated income
- Projections are conservative; if sales take off, the situation could change
- Amount drawn from reserves expected to be less than $200k, potentially repayable by May/June from parade and exhibitor revenues

Lopez also reported on the meeting with Treasurer Ford and a city supervisor, sharing that SF Pride would be interested in additional annual city support.

Additional updates:
- RFPs sent for production
- Community space survey distributed
- Staff preparing plans for the year
- Full budget expected for review soon

---

## Director Discussion

Director Smith thanked Fred and shared concerns:

1. The city and state are not shutting down, placing moral responsibility on SF Pride to make right decisions for challenged communities
2. Avoiding the perception that Black/Brown women's stages will take a hit due to funding
3. How can we leverage city connections through Carolyn, Lisa Williams, or George Smith?
4. Would like monthly sponsorship reports with goals vs. actuals
5. Exploring demographic targeting for community stages that could benefit stages, sponsors, and the organization

Executive Director Lopez responded:
- The community deserves a celebration
- Confident and excited about the community spaces program
- Ten opportunities for stages, matching 2019
- Criteria centers Black and Brown women, trans people, gender non-conforming people, two-spirited individuals
- "It may look different than before, and that isn't necessarily bad—it is evidence of us growing and changing and evolving"

President Wysinger noted the organization has improved at sharing its sponsor story and keeping community safety at the forefront.
