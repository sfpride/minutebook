## Agency Updates

Interim Executive Director Suzanne Ford presented agency updates at 7:13 PM:

- **Budget & Finance:** The committee met this week and will meet in person on September 20 to review the budget. The process is collaborative and ideas are welcome. The Board will approve the budget in November.
- **Production Proposals:** Next week, JJ-LA will meet with SF Pride to walk the footprint, meet the team, and work on a production proposal. BD+20 will also meet with the team and prepare a production proposal.
- **Upcoming Fall Events:**
  - **Annual General Meeting (AGM):** Saturday, September 10; Chris Grafton is lead.
  - **Gratitude Party:** September 17 at The Eagle; Marsha Levine is lead. Carolyn Wysinger will speak as part of the program. Volunteers assisting with setup are asked to arrive by 12:30 PM.

**Motion to extend time:** President Wysinger moved to add 10 minutes to this agenda item; seconded by Director Joshua Smith.  
**Vote:** Ayes: 6, Noes: 0, Abstain: 0. *Motion passed.*

- **Latinx Community Event:** September 30 at El Rio, led by Manuel Alejandro Perez and Maceo Persson. The goal is building relationships and community connections between Pride and the Latinx community. The event may include an MPX vaccination booth. Marketing begins this week, leading to a calendar of events in the spring.
- **Fleet Week Event:** September 30 in the Castro; Suzanne Ford is lead. TurnOut will be contracted for volunteer support.
- **Golf Awards Event:** October 7 at the Fairmont, 6:00 PM – 9:00 PM. Board members have tickets and are encouraged to attend.
- **Golf Tournament:** October 8 at 11:00 AM at Harding Park, with dinner following the tournament.
- **Ken Jones Awards:** October 20; will need board support. The Mayor has been asked to select this year's Teddy Witherington Award recipient.

## New Business

### Board Transparency
Vice President Pham led a 10-minute discussion regarding transparency and how to effectively communicate decisions, processes, and information from the Executive Committee to the full Board, and among the full Board in general. Proposals included sending email updates from committees more regularly and encouraging communication between meetings.

### Non-Voting Membership
Director Reid requested to add an item to the agenda regarding activating a non-voting membership by board resolution. It was noted that this should go through Policies & Procedures before coming to the full Board, having already gone through Community Affairs and Development.

*Consensus achieved at 7:50 PM to work on a board resolution.*
