## Committee Reports

### Development Committee
- Secretary Lanyon and Director Collins acknowledged that 80% of give/get commitments have been met.
- The remainder of their time was ceded to Director Perez and Director Persson to give a brief verbal report on the Queerazon event:
  - Highlighted the major success of the event.
  - Identified needs for future events: prospectus, timeline, budget, and clarity on the stipends process.
  - Noted strong interest from attendees in connecting with SF Pride, along with confusion regarding cost for membership dues and what membership entails.

### Community Affairs Committee
Director Joshua Smith provided a rundown of committee activities:
- Boosting interactions with members.
- Potential member meet-and-greet with new board members.
- Planning a November holiday mixer for members.
- Committee consensus reached to hold quarterly mixers.
- A membership survey is currently in the works.

---

## Executive Director's Report

Executive Director Ford reviewed her written report:
- Expressed thanks to staff for assistance with Fall events: Queerazon, Gratitude party, golf events, and the Ken Jones Awards.
- Provided a brief overview of fiscal year-end balances and upcoming expenses and revenue.
- SF Pride will begin reimbursing expenses with Grants for the Arts (GFTA).
- Sponsorship sales and outreach for 2023 have begun.
