## Officer Reports

### President's Report
President Wysinger gave a verbal report which was also included in the board packet. The last week was very busy and specific updates will be addressed later in the meeting. Board members were reminded to check their email this month for updates and announcements.

### Vice President's Report
At 7:17 PM, Vice President Pham gave a verbal report which was also included in the board packet:
- Expressed gratitude to the board for trusting the Executive Committee.
- Expressed pride in the culture created and reinforced as a board, noting that disagreements can occur without being disagreeable.
- **Membership Update:** 30 new members joined via Cheer SF, serving as a great example of year-round engagement (Pride 365).

### Treasurer's Report
At 7:20 PM, Treasurer Luster gave a verbal report which was also included in the board packet:
- The organization continues to see strong financials and will see an increase in spending this month.
- The Interim Executive Director continues to secure commitments from partners, and a line of credit remains available.

## Committee Reports

### Budget & Finance Committee
No report.

### Policies and Procedures Committee
No report. This committee will be reconvened in July (post-celebration).

### Development Committee
Presented by Development Co-Chair Collins:
- Expressed thanks to everyone who supported the Club Fugazi event, noting it was a great event and opportunity for the community.
- Raised $15,500 at the event.
- Additional details will be provided in July.

### Community Affairs Committee
Presented by CAC Co-Chair Smith and Co-Chair Luster:
- **Membership Mixer:** Planned for June 15th at Beaux; board members were encouraged to attend.
- In July, CAC will host a space for member feedback and will look ahead to plan the remainder of the year.
- **Resistance Contingent Update (presented by Co-Chair Luster):** Currently 22 groups are signed up. A poster-making session is scheduled at Fluid Co-Op on June 23rd.

---

## Agency Updates

Interim Executive Director Suzanne Ford presented agency updates:
- Board name tags have been ordered and will be available soon.
- The Parade and Celebration is 16 days away.
- 200 contingents are currently signed up for the Parade, which is fewer than the 310 contingents in 2019. Funds have been raised for nonprofits as part of the new model for parade contingents.
- The Main Stage headliner is Martha Wash.
- 125 volunteers are still needed for the weekend.
- Presented an overview of the weekend and roles for board members who will receive credentials and all-access passes.
- Presented the calendar of events.
- **Financial Update:** SF Pride received a commitment from Kaiser, as they are particularly interested in the organization's equity work and will be supporting ongoing discussions with the Pride Alliance. To date, $1.728M in funds has been confirmed against a $2M goal, leaving a $450k gap. Further financial discussion will occur during closed session.
