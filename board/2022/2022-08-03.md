## Officer Reports

### President's Report
President Carolyn Wysinger gave a verbal report noting that this month is focused on event wrap-up and personnel:
- Attended the thank you party hosted by Donna & Gary for PRC.
- Attended the *Bay Times* thank you party, extending gratitude to board members and staff who attended.
- Thanked Director Maceo Persson for reaching out and stepping up to help support Pride, and the event with Director Manuel Alejandro Perez.

### Vice President's Report
Vice President Nguyen Pham noted his written report was included in the meeting packet.

### Treasurer's Report
Treasurer Janelle Luster noted her written report was included in the meeting packet, highlighting that the organization is still collecting sponsorships and donations while continuing to pay bills from the celebration.

### Secretary's Report
Secretary Elizabeth Lanyon had no report.

---

## Executive Director's Report

Interim Executive Director Suzanne Ford presented an overview and update:
- Financial overview and update presented.
- Event wrap-up is ongoing.
- Staffing updates presented.
- Calendar of events presented for the fall, all of which will require board support and involvement.
- Staff will attend InterPride in October.
- **Ask to the Board:** Kaiser Permanente is interested in supporting community discussions and town halls with different segments of the community. Board members are asked to send ideas on implementation to Suzanne Ford and the Executive Committee.
- Director Joshua Smith asked whether volunteers under 21 years of age would be admitted to the Volunteer Gratitude Party at the Eagle.
- Marsha Levine will contact the Eagle and provide an update; Eagle Plaza (the park just outside the Eagle) is also an option.

## Committee Reports

### Budget & Finance Committee
No report.

### Policies & Procedures Committee
Director Manuel Alejandro Perez presented an update regarding Policies & Procedures, with an outline for the review process included in the board packet. The committee recommends an annual cycle of review of organizational documents, including a presented schedule through Spring 2023.

**Motion:** Move to approve the recommended review as presented by the Policies & Procedures Committee.  
**Motion:** Director Joshua Smith  
**Second:** Vice President Nguyen Pham  
**Vote:** Ayes: 7, Noes: 0. Motion passed at 7:32 PM.

### Development Committee
No report; the committee meeting is scheduled for tomorrow, Thursday.

### Community Affairs Committee
Director Joshua Smith presented the report:
- The August meeting will focus on the Annual General Meeting (AGM).
- The September meeting will provide an opportunity to introduce new board members.
- The October meeting will be a mixer event.
- Member outreach is being conducted via email blasts and the newsletter.
- Director Di'ara Reid is working on a new member welcome message.
