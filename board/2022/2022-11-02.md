## New Business

### Kaiser Panel
Interim Executive Director Ford reported on ongoing meetings with Kaiser over the last couple of months stemming from the 2022 agreement (in part due to SF Pride's handling of SFPD negotiations), noting Kaiser is a sponsor. Kaiser would like SF Pride to submit a proposal for a panel to discuss Policing at the intersections of Race and Class, and requested Board feedback.

- Director Lanyon suggested engaging community folks first before eventually discussing again with SFPD and first responders. She also recommended utilizing a trained facilitator (offering two suggestions) and stated willingness to work on the budget with Interim Executive Director Ford.
- **Consensus:** Reached at 9:06 PM to move forward with the Kaiser RFP, with Interim Executive Director Ford leading.

### Protocol and Respect
Director Martley-Jordan shared reflections regarding the previous meeting with the production company, noting perceived body language and hand gestures in response to suggestions, feeling out of place, and feeling that her opinion did not matter.
- President Pham expressed empathy and apologized for her experience.
- 10 minutes were added to the discussion time.
- Director J. Smith noted that hybrid meetings (half remote, half in-person) present challenges.
- Director Martley-Jordan emphasized the importance of being in spaces where members can agree, disagree, and agree to disagree, acknowledging that all members enter the work with their own experiences and baggage.
- Director Persson noted challenges with meeting start times, late nights, and staying on timeline.

## Closed Session

The public session was adjourned at 8:25 PM, with board members entering closed session at 8:35 PM.

### Production
Interim Executive Director Ford presented updates regarding event production:
- JJLA received glowing reviews from LA Pride.
- Ticketing response from JJLA was provided for Board review.
- Interim Executive Director Ford requested a temperature check on moving forward with JJLA, with the possibility of utilizing BD+20 for permitting.

**Director Discussion:**
- Director Reid stated a desire for more information from BD+20, expressing a feeling that JJLA was favored from the outset.
- Director J. Smith stated readiness to move forward, emphasizing the need to ensure local representation.
- Director Lanyon expressed concern that JJLA is similar to past producers (Cogs and Marvel/Scott) and noted worries about them not being local, as well as potential conflicts with producing an event on June 1 alongside SF Pride. She appreciated Elliot's (BD+20) connections and emphasized the need to improve City relationships.
- Interim Executive Director Ford shared reservations regarding BD+20's capacity to execute SF Pride.

**Action:** Consensus was reached at 10:22 PM for Interim Executive Director Ford to move forward in discussions with JJLA.
