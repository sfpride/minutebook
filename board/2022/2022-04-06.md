## Officer Reports

### President's Report
President Wysinger thanked fellow board members, sharing that their humor and support helped turn around a difficult day. She noted the ongoing challenges of working in a world where people of color are still treated as "the help."

Highlighting recent progress, President Wysinger stated:
> *"It's hard to do all these fabulous things, be on ABC7 with our Interim Executive Director announcing an amazing media broadcast partnership that, according to a former board president, is the type of collaborative partnership they had in mind when they first endeavored to get a media partnership."*

She noted that Interim Executive Director Ford would expand on the partnership and affirmed that SF Pride will continue to center community and preserve the organization's culture.

### Vice President's Report
Vice President Pham verbally summarized his written report, noting numerous recent meetings and appearances. He expressed appreciation for the mutual respect and understanding within the Executive Committee and with the Interim Executive Director, noting he felt very engaged. He reiterated the importance of transparency and noted he remains open to feedback and constructive criticism.

### Treasurer's Report
Treasurer Luster provided a brief verbal review of her written report in the packet, thanking Interim Executive Director Ford for assistance during the transition into the budget and finance process. She noted that expenses have remained low, though increases are anticipated soon due to production costs. (A full Budget and Finance update was reserved for closed session.)

### Secretary's Report
Secretary Lanyon had no formal report beyond the approval of the February and March minutes.

---

## Executive Director's Report

Interim Executive Director Suzanne Ford presented a verbal overview of agency updates:

- Expressed gratitude for the enormous task ahead, calling it overwhelming but full of joy.
- Thanked Gary Virginia and hostess Donna Sachet for the membership drive event at the Lookout in the Castro, which raised over $3,500.
- Attended the hearing and confirmed that the ISCOTT permit has been approved.
- Parade and exhibitor registrations are increasing substantially; media appearances with President Wysinger have had a strong positive impact. Some registrations carry over from previous fiscal years.
- Held a transition and hand-off meeting with former parade manager Mike, incoming parade manager Elycia, and Community Relations Manager Marsha Levine.
- **Main Stage:** Jenn Stokes is meeting weekly to program acts for the Main Stage under an agreed-upon smaller budget; artist announcements expected in the coming weeks.
- All 8 community stages have been handed off to production.
- Locations have been secured for Community Space activations, including Faerie Freedom Village, Leather Alley, and Country Western Dance.
- Weekly staff and contractor meetings have resumed following full team onboarding.
- Approximately $744k under contract for firm commitments from corporate partners.
- San Francisco Grants for the Arts current grant was increased by ~$75k; awaiting disbursement of funds.
- Summarized plans for a potential fundraising collaboration with Club Fugazi.

**Director Q&A:**
- *Director Smith* inquired about sponsor commitments and cash-inflow timelines. Interim ED Ford responded: *"Cashflow is coming in quick, everybody knows we up against it,"* noting no anticipated issues chasing receivables.
- *Director Smith* noted an inquiry from a past producer of the Tantra stage and agreed to follow up with Interim ED Ford offline.

## Committee Reports

### Development Committee
Secretary Lanyon (Co-Chair) reminded the Board of the give/get obligation. She and Co-Chair Collins invited members to discuss individual fundraising plans. Director Smith suggested exploring a benefit event hosted by Comfort & Joy.

### Audit Committee
Vice President Pham reported that committee activity is paused during the production cycle and will resume in 3 to 4 months.

### Community Affairs Committee
Director Smith reported:
- TurnOut will present at the April membership meeting; outreach to Dykes on Bikes is underway for May.
- The June membership meeting may be canceled to encourage attendance at SF Pride's partnership event with the African American Art & Culture Complex (AAACC).
- Collaborating with the Policies & Procedures Committee to review membership policies.
- Organizing upcoming Member Mixers, including a potential May event at the Lookout hosted by Gary Virginia and Donna Sachet.

### Resistance Contingent
Treasurer Luster reported that invitations to participate in the Resistance Contingent have been distributed. Fluid Co-Op offered space at La Cocina for poster-making and community organizing. Sign-ups are live at [sfpride.org/resist](https://sfpride.org/resist).

### Policies & Procedures Committee
Director Perez reported that the committee is partnering with Community Affairs to review membership policies. A structured 2-year rolling review cycle for all organizational policies is being established, with mechanisms for urgent out-of-cycle reviews. The next committee meeting is scheduled for Wednesday, April 20, 2022.

*Discussion:* Director Smith asked if relevant files had been received to share with members. Staff member Chris confirmed the documents were received and would be uploaded to the membership site and meeting agendas. Director Perez requested copies be forwarded to him directly.

### Other Committees
Personnel, Long Range Planning, and Nominating committees had no reports.
