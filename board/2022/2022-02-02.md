# Board Meeting — February 2, 2022
President of the Board Carolyn Wysinger called the meeting of the **San Francisco Lesbian, Gay, Bisexual, Transgender Pride Parade and Celebration Committee** to order at 7:03pm on the evening of February 2nd **2022** over Zoom.

President Wysinger called for attendees to introduce themselves. In attendance were:

1.  Carolyn Wysinger, President
2.  Suzanne Ford, Treasurer
3.  Di'ara Reid, Director
4.  Maceo Persson, Director
5.  Elizabeth Lanyon, Secretary
6.  Joshua Smith, Director
7.  Manuel Perez, Director
8.  Chris Grafton, Staff / Minutes
9.  Marsha Levine, Staff, Community Affiliates Lead
10. Fred Lopez, Staff, Executive Director
11. Janelle Luster, Director
12. Spring Collins, Director
13. Anjali Rimi, Director
14. RJB, Staff, Director of Partnerships and Giving
15. Nguyen Pham, Vice President (late)

Quorum was confirmed at 7:03pm

Apologies were sent from Directors Perez who could not be present; Director Persson was absent.

An acknowledgement that SF Pride meetings are held on the unceded ancestral homeland of the Ramaytush Ohlone people was read by Director Smith.

President Wysinger wished everyone a Happy Lunar New Year, and invited everyone to celebrate Black History Month, indeed in February but also every day.

Meeting roles were appointed with Treasurer Ford serving as vibe watch, RJB monitoring stack, and Director Smith keeping time.

Secretary Lanyon read the mission statement, the other board members joining in for the final words, "\...liberate our people".

Treasurer Ford read the financial protocol.

President Wysinger calls for a motion to add approval of the agenda to the Agenda, so moved by Director Smith and seconded by Treasurer Ford, approved unanimously by those present, with all Directors voting "aye".

President Wysinger called for a motion to approve the agenda, made by Secretary Lanyon, and seconded by Director Reid. The agenda was approved unanimously, with all Directors present voting "aye" at 7:10pm.

President Wysinger noted that Vice President Pham sent apologies, that he would be late to the meeting.

President Wysinger called for a motion to approve the minutes from the meetings of the Board of Directors for December 2021 and January 2022, so made by Director Smith, and seconded by Director Collins, approved unanimously by those present, with all Directors voting "aye" at 7:11pm.

President Wysinger called for members of the board to share announcements, and hearing none she gave her President's Report, noting that:

- The Board held a successful retreat the weekend before the Board meeting.

- A meeting was held with the Pride Alliance, hopeful to have an update in the coming weeks.

- Carolyn sat in on a production meeting with Scott and Fred, it was a good opportunity to learn more about the day to day of production.

- Shout out to the Commonwealth Club who hosted the retreat, and thanks to Sap Ver for hosting the board for a happy hour. Our Trans board members were made to feel a bit uncomfortable at the RIU at fisherman's warf, but it is a learning opportunity for the hotel.

- Represented the organization at the almost-official announcement of the event on KRON, letting everyone know there would be some sort of celebration in June.

- Represented the org when speaking to Amy Schneider at the commonwealth club.

A reminder that having any sort of event concluded President Wysinger's President's report.

President Wysinger noted that the Vice President's report was included in the packet, and shared his apologies that he would be late to the meeting.

Treasurer Ford apologized for the short time the board was given her report before the meeting, reminding everyone that the financial data for the preceding month is available in the afternoon of the first, allowing limited time to produce it before the regular Board meeting.

Treasurer Ford elaborated upon her prepared report saying the executive committee has had a high level discussion of the budget, obstacles the organization faces, cash-flow issues.

Secretary Lanyon had no report, calling for feedback on the new style of minutes.

Executive Director Lopez shared praise for all those involved in producing board retreat, noting that Chris would reach out to Board members to schedule time to follow up on IT needs. He expanded upon the drawdown from reserve and lines of credit

Due in part to the current situation of rising covid cases, Executive Director Lopez noted that opening sales now for an event 5 months away doesn't seem like the right move. Referencing to the cash flow forecast included in the meeting packet, he noted that soon the organization will need to take advantage of its reserve account, its line of credit, or perhaps some other source of revenue. "February and March", he noted, "are going to be challenging because we have more expenses than anticipated income." He explained that the projections are very conservative, and if sales take off once the registration sites open, then that situation could change. He outlined the pros and cons of each source of funds, anticipating that the amount drawn down from any would be less than \$200k, and based on parade and exhibitor revenues we may be able to start paying that back by May or June.

Executive Director Lopez expanded on the meeting that he and Treasurer Ford had held with a city supervisor, saying they explained the status of the organization in broad strokes, and shared that SF Pride would be interested in additional support from the city, possibly on an annual basis.

Fred mentioned that RFPs had gone out on the production side of things, the community space survey has been sent out, and the staff have been getting their plans ready for the year, and we can expect a full budget to review and pass pretty soon.

Director Smith thanked Fred for his work and shared a few of his concerns:

1.  It's clear that the city and state are not shutting down so the moral compass leans on SF Pride to make the right decisions for our more challenged communities, in terms of health.

2.  Avoiding the perception that black/brown women's stages and venues will take a hit because we don't have enough money to do what we want to do.

3.  How can we engage Carolyn, Lisa Willams, or George Smith who are very connected with the city? How can we leverage those relationships?

4.  Would like to see monthly sponsorship reports, monthly goals as compared to what is coming in.

5.  "Looking forward to seeing if there are any opportunities in which we can leverage the targeting of our demographics through community stages and venues to see if there are opportunities that would benefit the stages because a certain sponsor wants to be in on that demographic, and the city, or our org can benefit from it as well as the stage. "

Executive Director Lopez responded by saying that he felt the community absolutely deserved a celebration, and from his point of view we should still be in decent shape in 5 months. He shared that he feels confident and excited about the community spaces program, sharing that there would be ten opportunities for stages, meeting the same number from 2019. He noted that everything would look different from 2019, but the criteria and the process being used is "the centering of black and brown women, trans people, gender non-conforming people, the two-spirited, etc. It may look different than before, and that isn't necessarily bad, it is evidence of us growing and changing and evolving in the situation we've been given."

President Wysinger shared that we've been really bad about sharing our sponsor story in the past, and we have done better in the last two years about sharing our story and keeping the safety of the community at the front of our mind.

Director Smith shared the reason for his questions being another organization he is involved in has had conversations weighing the importance of maintaining \[queer\] spaces given that people have been vaccinated and boosted and continued concerns about the ongoing pandemic.

Vice President Pham made a report of the Audit committee, noting that preliminary audit findings for the fiscal year 2020 were being reviewed by the committee, and barring further committee questions, the audit team will be authorized to proceed with a full audit.

Treasurer Ford noted that the Budget and Finance committee has not met. Executive Director Lopez noted that a preliminary budget will be available in two weeks, and Treasurer Ford said she would schedule a meeting in about 2 and a half weeks.

President Wysinger answered a question from Director Spring, noting that each committee would have meeting time at the upcoming board retreat.

President Wysinger reminded community affairs committee members to check for an email to schedule that committee's first meeting. Director Smith shared that OpenHouse would like to collaborate, and that once a date had been selected he would share items that remained tabled from the previous year.

Returning to no Old Business, hearing no New Business, and with no members of the public present to make comment, the meeting was closed in memory of:

1.  Betty White (Carolyn Wysinger) - American actress, comedian, author, and animal welfare advocate

2.  Richard Magary (Fred Lopez) - Longtime Administrator of Castro Merchants

The public session of the meeting was adjourned by President Wysinger at 8:03pm with board members remaining online for a closed session to follow.

Closed session was adjourned at 8:23 pm
