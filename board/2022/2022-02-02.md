## Executive Director's Report

Executive Director Lopez shared praise for everyone involved in producing the board retreat, noting that Chris Grafton would reach out to board members to schedule follow-ups on IT needs. He expanded on financial forecasts:

- Due in part to rising COVID cases, opening sales now for an event 5 months away does not seem like the right move.
- Referencing the cash flow forecast in the packet, SF Pride may soon need to utilize its reserve account, line of credit, or other revenue sources. February and March will be challenging due to expenses exceeding anticipated income.
- Projections remain conservative; if sales accelerate once registration opens, the situation could improve. Drawdowns are anticipated to be under $200k, with potential repayment beginning by May or June from parade and exhibitor revenues.
- Expanded on a meeting held with Treasurer Ford and a city supervisor, having outlined the organization's status in broad strokes and expressing SF Pride's interest in additional support from the City, possibly on an annual basis.
- Production RFPs have been issued, the community space survey has been distributed, and staff are preparing annual plans. A full budget will be presented soon for review and approval.

---

## Director Discussion

Director Smith thanked Executive Director Lopez and raised five points:
1. Since the city and state are not shutting down, the moral compass leans on SF Pride to make the right health decisions for more challenged communities.
2. Avoiding the perception that Black and Brown women's stages and venues will take a hit due to financial constraints.
3. Exploring how to engage and leverage relationships with the City through Carolyn Wysinger, Lisa Williams, or George Smith.
4. Requesting monthly sponsorship reports showing monthly goals compared to actual incoming revenue.
5. Exploring opportunities to leverage demographic targeting through community stages and venues to benefit the stages, sponsors, the City, and SF Pride.

Executive Director Lopez responded:
- The community absolutely deserves a celebration, and the organization should be in decent shape in 5 months.
- Confident and excited about the community spaces program, noting there will be 10 opportunities for stages (matching 2019 levels).
- While execution will differ from 2019, the criteria and process center Black and Brown women, trans people, gender non-conforming people, and two-spirited individuals: *"It may look different than before, and that isn't necessarily bad, it is evidence of us growing and changing and evolving in the situation we've been given."*

President Wysinger noted that SF Pride has improved over the last two years in sharing its sponsor story while keeping community safety at the forefront.

Director Smith clarified that his questions arose from discussions at another organization weighing the importance of maintaining queer spaces amidst vaccinations, boosters, and ongoing pandemic concerns.
