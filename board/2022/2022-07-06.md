## Officer Reports

### President's Report
President Carolyn Wysinger gave a verbal report at 7:16 PM:
- Expressed gratitude to everyone for Pride weekend, sharing positive reflections and noting there were no incidents on the Parade Route.
- Noted that many board members were out sick and were missed.
- Thanked leaders who stepped up to help over the weekend, including Scott and Suzanne, the Women's March and Sophia, and commended the PR team for being professional and thorough.
- Provided updates regarding an incident at the end of the celebration, noting it would be discussed further in closed session.
- Thanked all who supported fundraising efforts, including events at Club Fugazi and City Hall.
- Looked ahead to the upcoming Annual General Meeting (AGM) and posed a reflective question to the board: *"What have you stepped up to do for Pride the organization?"*

### Vice President's Report
Vice President Nguyen Pham gave a verbal report at 7:25 PM, which was also provided as a written report in the board packet.

### Treasurer's Report
Treasurer Janelle Luster gave a verbal report at 7:30 PM, which was also provided as a written report in the board packet.

### Secretary's Report
Secretary Elizabeth Lanyon had no additional formal report at 7:32 PM, but shared appreciation for Carly's work on partnership development. President Wysinger added thanks to Xavier for social media work, emphasizing the importance of maintaining and growing these staff roles.

---

## Executive Director's Report

Interim Executive Director Suzanne Ford gave a verbal report at 7:33 PM:
- SF Pride secured a total of 43 sponsors this year.
- Commended the Parade Manager for amazing work.
- BART recorded its highest ridership since March 2020.
- Communications throughout the event were cohesive.
- Noted the need to think creatively about Saturday's event programming to increase attendance.
- Overall funding was very strong, and the organization is continuing to collect additional revenue from beverage sales.

**Discussion & Comments:**
- Director Perez commented that the Celebration and Parade provided a path forward that felt whole and honored our community's spirit.
- Director Luster noted she watched the event from home.
- Chris Grafton commended Interim Executive Director Ford for her leadership in making the event happen, highlighting her success with fundraising and political relationships.
